Education & Mission

About DinoBase

🦴

Our Mission

DinoBase is a passion project dedicated to making paleontology accessible to everyone — from curious children to serious researchers. Every species is documented with scientific accuracy.

🔬

Scientific Accuracy

All data is sourced from peer-reviewed paleontological research. We include uncertainty ranges where exact measurements are debated among scientists.

🌍

Global Coverage

Fossil discoveries from every continent are mapped and documented, reflecting the truly global nature of the prehistoric world.

📚

Continuous Updates

Paleontology is a living science. DinoBase is updated as new discoveries rewrite what we know about the Mesozoic Era.
